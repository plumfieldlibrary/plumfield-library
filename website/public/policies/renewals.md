# Renewals

## Policy overview

Plumfield Library does not offer renewals at this time. With so many readers enjoying our books, keeping them circulating is one of the most important ways we steward what we have. The 45-day loan period gives each family a generous window with their books, and returning them on time means the next family gets their turn.

## Key points

- Renewals are not offered while the library maintains fair circulation for all families.
- Patrons may return a book and check it out again if no one else is waiting for it.
- The library's software and multi-location tracking make managed renewals impractical for the small team.
- New mothers may keep books for up to three months during maternity leave with prior arrangement.
