# Renewals

## Policy overview

Plumfield Library does not offer renewals at this time. This policy explains why renewals are unavailable and notes a special accommodation for new mothers.

## Key points

- Renewals are not offered due to software and circulation limitations.
- The library needs to keep books circulating across multiple locations.
- New mothers may keep books for up to three months during maternity leave with prior arrangement.
