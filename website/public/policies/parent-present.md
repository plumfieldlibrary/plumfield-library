# Parent-Present Policy

## Policy overview

Plumfield Living Library requires parents, guardians, or responsible adults to remain present and available during any library class, gathering, or visit unless otherwise stated in writing.

## Key points

- Parents or responsible adults must stay on site for the full duration of Plumfield activities.
- This policy applies at all locations, including outdoor areas and branch spaces.
- Children age 5 and under need close support throughout activities.
- Plumfield may ask families to step out or withdraw if a child’s needs become unsafe or disruptive.
