# Volunteers

## Policy overview

Volunteering and internships are temporarily paused as Plumfield expands into new branches and launches new programs.

## Key points

- New volunteer applications are currently on hold.
- The library hopes to reopen the internship program next school year.
- This pause helps Plumfield focus on stable operations during a period of transition.
