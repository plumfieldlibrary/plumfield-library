# Volunteers

## Policy overview

Plumfield Library is temporarily pausing new volunteer and internship applications as the library grows, launches new programs, and brings more than 30 books back into print. This pause helps the team train and welcome volunteers well rather than in the midst of transition.

## Key points

- New volunteer applications and internships are currently on hold.
- The pause allows the team to focus on growth and stable operations.
- Plumfield is prayerfully discerning the best way to train and support volunteers before relaunching the program.
